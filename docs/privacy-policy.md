# Venue Wrangler Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 23, 2026

Venue Wrangler collects:
- Account data: name, email, role, venue assignment
- Location data: precise GPS location when a user opens the Time Clock screen and taps refresh / clock in / clock out
- Device data: push token for notifications
- Operational data entered by venue staff, including schedules, reservations, guest notes, floor/table status, bar stock counts, uploaded inventory files/photos, payroll export data, and manager goals

Venue Wrangler uses location data to:
- Verify the user is physically inside the venue geofence before allowing clock-in or clock-out
- Display the clock-in location to managers for audit and review

Venue Wrangler uses account and operational data to:
- Provide venue operations tools such as scheduling, time clock, floor management, reservations, inventory, messaging, reporting, billing, and notifications
- Send schedule updates, shift changes, and venue alerts
- Support customer service, security, and compliance

Venue Wrangler does not:
- Sell personal data
- Share personal or location data with third parties except the service providers required to operate the app

Service providers (subprocessors):
- Google Cloud and Supabase - application hosting and database
- Apple In-App Purchase and Stripe - subscription payments
- Amazon Web Services (S3) - photo and document storage
- Resend - transactional email
- Sentry - error diagnostics
- Google Gemini - AI features, including parsing an uploaded staff roster, which can include names, email addresses, phone numbers and job titles

Data retention:
- Time entries and location proofs are retained for operational and compliance purposes
- Users may request deletion subject to legal retention obligations
