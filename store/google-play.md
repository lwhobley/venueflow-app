- Short description: Venue ops app with geofenced staff clock-in.
- Full description: Run your venue from one coordinated workspace. Venue Wrangler brings staff scheduling, geofenced time clock, reservations, floor and table operations, shift communication, checklists, inventory, guest management, and manager reporting together for restaurants, bars, hotels, arenas, stadiums, and event teams. Owners and managers can publish schedules, monitor live operations, coordinate staff, and keep service information current. Team members can view shifts, manage availability, communicate with coworkers, and clock in or out when they are at the venue. Role-based access keeps management and staff tools appropriately separated.
